Privacy policy

IPTV+ is a personal app. It has no servers of its own and collects no data about you.

Google Drive sync (optional)

  • If you sign in with Google, the app asks only for the drive.file permission. This lets it read and write the one small file it creates itself in your own Google Drive (iptvplus-sync.json). It cannot see, change or delete any of your other files.
  • That file holds your favorites, watchlist, watched titles and playback positions. It is used only to keep your own devices in sync.
  • The sign-in token stays on your device. Nothing is sent to anyone else, sold or shared.
  • To stop syncing, sign out in the app's settings, or remove IPTV+ at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also delete iptvplus-sync.json from your Drive at any time.

Everything else

Your playlist details and settings are stored only on your device.
